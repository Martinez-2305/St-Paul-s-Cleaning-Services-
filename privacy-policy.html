Privacy & Cookie Policy

What information we collect when you contact us, why we need it, and how we look after it.

Who we are

This website belongs to St Paul's Cleaning Services, a domestic cleaning business based in Epsom, Surrey. We are responsible for the personal information you give us through this website. You can reach us on +44 7930 405972, by email at contact@stpaulscleaningservices.co.uk, or through our contact page.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote using the form on our website, we collect:

  • your first and last name
  • your email address
  • your phone number
  • the service you are interested in
  • anything you write in the message box, such as your address or details about your home

If you call, email or WhatsApp us, we keep the details you share so we can reply and arrange your clean.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, give you a quote, and arrange and carry out the cleaning you book. We do not send marketing emails, and we never sell or rent your information to anyone.

Our legal basis under UK GDPR is that we need this information to respond to your request and to take steps towards a contract with you. Once you book, we use it to provide the service.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of trusted services to run this website:

  • Formspree receives the quote form and forwards it to us by email. Formspree is based in the United States and processes data under standard data protection contract terms.
  • Vercel hosts this website. Like any web host, it records basic technical information such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep the site secure.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this website. Your browser connects to Google to download them, which shares your IP address with Google.
  • WhatsApp (owned by Meta) carries any messages you send us through the WhatsApp button. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to those messages.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to deal with your request. If you become a customer, we keep your booking records for as long as we need them to provide the service and meet UK tax and accounting rules.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, so there is nothing to accept or turn off. If we ever add cookies, we will update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. To make a request, email contact@stpaulscleaningservices.co.uk, call us, or use our contact page. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
